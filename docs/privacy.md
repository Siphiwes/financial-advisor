# Privacy and data handling plan

The prototype should use synthetic data only.

Before collecting personal or financial information, document:

- The purpose and lawful basis for each data category.
- User notice, consent where required, and how users can access, correct or delete their data.
- Data minimization, retention periods and secure deletion.
- Who can access records and how access is audited.
- Whether data is sent to external AI providers, what is sent, and how provider retention is controlled.
- Storage location, cross-border processing considerations, backups and incident handling.

Review the design against applicable South African privacy requirements, including POPIA, before onboarding real users. This document is a planning checklist, not legal advice or a compliance certification.
