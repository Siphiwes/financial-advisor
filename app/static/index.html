External AI data processing

When enabled, your question and calculated income, expense, cash-flow and category totals are sent to IBM watsonx.ai. Bank statement files, account numbers, transaction descriptions and references are not sent.

You can withdraw consent at any time. The AI advisor also remains disabled until the application operator enables and approves the integration.
